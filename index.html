A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Are SemiSocket stations open today?+

Not yet. SemiSocket is a network in development. We’re exploring fleet needs, site opportunities, and infrastructure partnerships. Station locations and opening dates will be announced when confirmed. Images on this site show the proposed station concept.

Who is SemiSocket being built for?+

Our focus is electric semi trucks and the fleets and drivers that operate them. The concept prioritizes tractor-trailer access, highway proximity, and a useful place for drivers to take a break.

Will Tesla Semi trucks be supported?+

We want to serve electric heavy-duty fleets, including operators considering Tesla Semi. Vehicle compatibility, connectors, and charging equipment will be confirmed as station designs and technical partnerships develop. SemiSocket is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Tesla or any vehicle manufacturer.

What charging speeds and prices are planned?+

High-power charging is central to the vision. Actual output, session performance, and pricing will depend on the equipment, vehicle, utility service, and site. We’ll publish confirmed specifications before stations open.

What makes a promising station location?+

We’re interested in locations near freight corridors with practical highway access, enough room for full tractor-trailers, and a viable path to electrical capacity. Each opportunity will need site, utility, and commercial evaluation.
